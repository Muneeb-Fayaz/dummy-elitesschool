Who We Are

About Our School

The Elite's Co-Ed School is one of Srinagar's most respected educational institutions, located on Gupt Ganga Ishber Road, offering quality CBSE education from primary through secondary levels.

Our curriculum is delivered by experienced, compassionate educators who go beyond textbooks to develop critical thinking, creativity, and character in every student.

We are a co-educational school that believes in equal opportunities for all — fostering confidence, teamwork, and leadership skills that prepare students for a rapidly changing world.
